## Why it matters

Low-contrast text is difficult to read for users with low vision or in bright environments. WCAG AA requires a 4.5:1 ratio for normal text.

## React examples

### Do: Use design tokens with sufficient contrast

```tsx
<p className="text-foreground bg-background">Readable body text</p>
```

### Don't: Use light gray on white

```tsx
<p className="text-gray-300 bg-white">Hard to read</p>
```

## How our analyzer checks this

The analyzer flags:

- **Inline styles** with statically known `color` and `backgroundColor` values that fall below 4.5:1 (3:1 for large text classes like `text-lg`).
- **Known failing Tailwind pairs** such as `text-gray-300` on `bg-white`.
- **Semantic design tokens** from `app/globals.css` (light theme only), such as `text-foreground` on `bg-background`, when the resolved ratio is below 4.5:1.
- **`cn()` / `clsx()` calls** with statically known arguments — dynamic conditions are skipped.

Dark mode token values are not inferred statically; verify dark-theme pairs manually or in the browser.

## Tips

- Test color pairs with a contrast checker before shipping.
- Don't rely on color alone to convey meaning — add text or icons too.
