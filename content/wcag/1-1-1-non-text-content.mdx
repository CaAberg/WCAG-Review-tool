## Why it matters

Screen readers cannot interpret images, icons, or charts without text alternatives. Users who cannot see visual content rely on `alt` text to understand what is being conveyed.

## React examples

### Do: Provide descriptive alt text

```tsx
<img src="/profile.jpg" alt="Jane Doe, product designer" />
```

### Do: Mark decorative images

```tsx
<img src="/divider.svg" alt="" role="presentation" />
```

### Don't: Omit alt entirely

```tsx
<img src="/chart.png" />
```

## How our analyzer checks this

The analyzer flags `<img>` elements missing an `alt` attribute and suggests adding descriptive text or marking the image as decorative.
